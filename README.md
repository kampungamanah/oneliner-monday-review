# oneliner-monday-review

## Entry

One original one-line review of my Monday: a meeting that starts before 9, coffee that gives up by 9:02, and an inbox full of unread emails that are all somehow my fault.

`main.ts` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Monday: my 9 a.m. meeting started at 8:58, my coffee was cold by 9:02, and my inbox had 47 unread emails — all of them somehow also my fault.

## How to run

    node main.ts

The entry lands in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set, otherwise in `/output/entry.txt`.
